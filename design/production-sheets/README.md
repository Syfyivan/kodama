# Production sprite sources

The seven `*-transparent.png` files are the retained production sheets used by the built-in desktop-pet families. They were produced with the built-in image generator from the approved concept sheets, rendered against a flat chroma key, converted to alpha, and normalized into 512×512 runtime sprites.

Shared prompt constraints: preserve the approved original character exactly; keep every form young and compact; use a perfectly flat chroma-key background with no text, border, floor, shadow, reflection, gradient, or watermark.

Pose order:

- `cottonpod-hermit`, `ferncurl-pangolin`, `rainpouch-newt`: egg, calm young, calm final form, focused work, curious waiting, genuinely happy completion, mild disappointment, peaceful sleep. Their default and working faces intentionally do not smile.
- `little-undo`: undo-key peek, young paper loop, final double loop, note-carrying work, warm-light sleep.
- `pocket-glider`: walnut peek, leaf-cuddling young glider, acorn-holding final form, acorn-sorting work, walnut sleep.
- `nuonuo-seal`: resting baby seal, happy young seal, final seaweed-holding form, seaweed-carrying work, round sleeping mochi.
- `upside-sprout`: sleeping seed, young sprout, final question-mark root, upside-down work, leaf-seat rest.
