# IP exploration archive

These images are the retained concept archive. The seven approved non-mouse designs are now wired into the desktop pet through normalized transparent production sprites; the active mouse assets remain unchanged.

Production chroma-key and transparent source sheets live in `../production-sheets/`. Runtime-ready 512×512 sprites live in `src/renderer/pets/<family>/` and can be regenerated with `scripts/extract-pet-sprites.py` after background removal.

## Final candidate roster

- `cottonpod-hermit-3d-behaviors-v2.png`: Cottonpod Hermit. The cotton shell absorbs spills, becomes heavy when wet, and sheds lint. The V2 sheet adds a balanced positive emotional range.
- `ferncurl-pangolin-2d-behaviors-v2.png`: Ferncurl Pangolin. Five simplified primary fern scales curl when watched and unfurl when the character feels safe.
- `rainpouch-newt-3d-behaviors-v2.png`: Rainpouch Newt. Its pitcher organ catches water and makes it wobble while delivering water to desk plants. The V2 sheet removes the chronically unhappy expression.
- `little-undo-2d-behaviors-v2.png`: Little Undo. A continuous paper ribbon that eats deleted fragments and returns the important ones. The V2 sheet softens the ribbon and simplifies the silhouette.
- `pocket-glider-3d-growth-v3.png`: Pocket Glider. Four child-sized forms with a curious, shy, focused, and quietly brave emotional progression; the final form remains close in size to the middle forms.
- `active-mouse-2d-behaviors-preview.png`: identity-preserving behavior preview for the active mouse: idle, thinking, eating, walking, and sleeping. This preview does not replace the running assets.
- `nuonuo-seal-2d-forms-v2.png`: NuoNuo Seal. A stable minimalist seal design with asymmetric whiskers, a single-side freckle mark, and five snack-related behavior forms.
- `upside-sprout-3d-behaviors-v2.png`: Upside Sprout. The root still grows upward and the cotyledons remain below, but the V2 palette uses light milk-tea lavender, vanilla, and pale sage instead of a dark seed coat.

This roster is intentionally closed for the current exploration round. Future work should optimize these eight characters rather than add more characters.

Original concepts should begin with a unique world rule, emotional contradiction, body behavior, and repeatable story engine before choosing a visual style or species.

## Growth rule

Characters should grow from very small to only slightly larger. Growth is expressed through posture, confidence, behavior, and one small prop rather than tall adult anatomy, bulk, wings, or power-up decoration.
